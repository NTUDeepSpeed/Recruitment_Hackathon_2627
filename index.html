NTU DeepSpeed · Recruitment Hackathon 26/27

Drive fast. Don’t crash.

Submissions closed on 18 October 2026. 27 teams entered across the two tracks and 20 finished. These two took the flag.

The brief

Write the software that drives a 1/10-scale racing car around a circuit it has never seen, as fast as it can, without hitting anything.

Two tracks, two simulators, one problem. Teams of three to five picked a track, wrote a driver and pushed it, and every push raced on GitHub Actions. Judging ran on one machine and took its times from the simulator’s own clock, so nobody’s laptop decided the result.

How it was scored

Score = 50×fastest lap of any teamyour fastest lap
+ 50×fastest 10-lap total of any teamyour 10-lap total
Collision+10 s on the lap, each. More than 10 is a disqualification.
Teams3 to 5 people, one team per person
JudgingOne machine, timed by the simulator’s clock
